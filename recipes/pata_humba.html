Pata Humba(Braised Pork Hocks)

Description

A braised pork dish that slowly cooked in a sweet and savory sauce.

Ingredients

  • Pork hocks
  • Cooking oil
  • White onion
  • Garlic
  • Salted black beans
  • Star anise
  • Dried bay leaves
  • Whole peppercorns
  • Soy sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Pineapple juice
  • Water
  • Brown sugar
  • Dried banana blossoms
  • Maggi Magic sarap/MSG

Steps

  1. Clean and Pre cook the pork. Boil pork hocks for about ten minutes to draw out impurities. Rinse well and drain.
  2. Brown for flavor. Heat cooking oil in a large pot. Fry pork hocks for about two minutes per side until lightly browned. Remove and set aside.
  3. Build the base. Using the remaining oil, saute garlic until it starts to turn to brown. Add onions and cook until soft and fragrant. Stir in salted beans and mash with a spoon. Add star anise, bay leaves, and whole peppercorns. Cook briefly to bloom the spices.
  4. Start the braise. Pour in soy sauce, vinegar, pineapple juice, and water. Return pork to the pot and bring to a gentle boil.
  5. Slow simmer until tender. Lower the heat, cover, and simmer until the pork is completely tender and the skin turns jiggly. Add small amounts of water as needed to keep the pork partially submerged.
  6. Finish and adjust to taste. Stir in brown sugar, banana blossooms. Simmer for about ten minutes to thicken and glaze. Season with maggi magic sarap or msg to taste. Serve how with plenty of sauce over rice.

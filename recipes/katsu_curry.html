Katsu Curry

Description

Japanese curry with breaded chicken cutlet.

Ingredients

  • Medium Onion, thinly sliced
  • Water
  • Vegetable oil
  • Curry roux/curry mix
  • Cooked rice
  • Chicken breast
  • All purpose flour
  • Egg, beaten
  • Panko, breadcrumbs
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Directions

  1. Stir-fry onions with oil in a large saucepan on medium heat for approx. 5min.
  2. Add water and bring to boil. Reduce heat, cover and simmer until ingredients are tender, approx. 15min.
  3. Turn the heat off, break Curry roux into pieces and add them to the saucepan. Stir until sauce mixes are completely melted. Simmer approx. 5min., stirring constantly.

    4. Katsu(Cutlet)

    1. Season chicken breast with flour, then dip into beaten egg. Coat with panko(breadcrumbs).
    2. In a large pan, heat vegetable oil to 170C(340 F). Drop a few panko into the pan to check oil temperature. (Panko should float on the surface and sizzle.
    3. Fry cutlets for 6-8 minutes, (depending on the thickness of meat). Don't put all ingredients at once; because it will cause the temperature to fall. Turn cutlets over a few times until panko becomes golden. Drain well.
  4. Place rice on a plate, top with cutlet, then serve curry sauce over.

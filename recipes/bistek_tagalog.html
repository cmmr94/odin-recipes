Bistek Tagalog

Description

Thinly sliced tender beef with savory and a little tangy taste from calamansi.

Ingredients

  • Thinly sliced beef
  • Soy sauce
  • Calamansi
  • Ground black pepper
  • White onion(sliced into rings)
  • Garlic
  • Water
  • Cooking Oil

Steps

  1. Marinate beef in soy sauce, calamnsi, and ground black pepper for at least 1 hour.(Note: marinate overnight for best results.)
  2. Heat the cooking oil in a pan then pan-fry half of the onions until the texture becomes soft. Set aside.
  3. Drain the marinade from the beef. Set aside the marinade. Pan-fry the beef on the same pan wehre the onions were fried for 1 minute per side. Remove from the pan. Set aside.
  4. Add more oil if needed. Saute garlic and remaining raw onions until onion softens.
  5. Pour the remaining marinade and water. Bring to a boil.
  6. Add beef. Cover the pan and simmer until meat is tender.(Note: add water as needed)
  7. Season with ground black pepper and salt as needed. Top with pan-fried onions.
  8. Transfer to a serving plate. Serve hot. Enjoy!
